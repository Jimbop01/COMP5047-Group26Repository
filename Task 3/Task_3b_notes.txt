FR-AO-3.4 (Incident & Disciplinary Governance): The subsystem shall centralize incident reports logged by wardens 
(e.g., noise complaints, safety breaches), allowing accommodation officers to initiate formal student disciplinary 
processes or welfare escalations.



Use case name: Incident & Disciplinary Governance
Participant(s): Wardens, Accommodation Staff
Entry condition(s): A warden has an incident to report
Exit condition(s): The incident is logged in a database for accommodation staff to reference and take appropriate action

Flow of events: 
1. The warden logs onto the system using their warden ID and password. 
2. The system checks the validity of the warden ID and password against its database. 
3. Login details are valid
4. The system loads the warden hub page. 
5. A warden selects "report an incident". 
6. The system displays a page containing a form for the warden to input details. 
7. The warden enters their warden ID, title for the incident, description, time/date, location, 
and optionally a photo or video of the incident. 
8. The system checks the validity of the warden ID, checks that the title and description of the
incident has been entered and isn't too long, checks that the date/time is not in the
future, and checks that the photo/video is below a specific size. 
9. The details are valid and are uploaded into the system. 
10. The incident is added to the "ongoing" category, and added to the list of incidents under
that warden's profile. 
11. Accommodation staff deals with the time-sensitive part of the incident. 
12. The incident is moved from "ongoing" to the "archive" category. 

Exceptional conditions and alternative flow of events:
3a. The warden enters invalid credentials. 
	1. Display "invalid credentials" and return to step 1. 
7a. The warden does not complete the form and instead decides to quit. 
	1. Discard the form and return to the warden hub page. 
7b. The warden uploads media. 
	1. Upload the media as linked to the form and continue to step 8. 
9a. Any of the warden ID, title, description, date/time, location, media is invalid. 
	1. Display error message pertaining to invalid field, return to step 7. 
Special requirements: 
1. Encryption should be used for the data traffic from the warden's personal device to the warden hub system. 
2. It should take no more than 15 seconds for the system to evaluate the validity of the form entry and
upload it to the system under a reasonable internet connection. 
3. Warden IDs are linked to the warden's personal details, e.g., name, work phone number and email
